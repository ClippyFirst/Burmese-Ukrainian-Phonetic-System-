# Competing analyses

## Prosody
Traditional work commonly describes four major-syllable tones with pitch, phonation, duration and vowel quality participating in the contrast. Duan & Zhu (2025) propose an eight-tone double-register analysis separating modal and breathy registers. These are retained separately.

## Vowel alternation
Mooney & Repetti-Ludlow (2021) argue that Burmese vowel alternations associated with tone/coda combinations can be explained through sonority sequencing rather than a direct tone-to-vowel rule. This is stored as an analysis claim, not as an unconditional rule.

## Tone/rime alternatives

Green's four-tone model allows only low, high and creaky categories in nasal rhymes. Mooney & Repetti-Ludlow (2021) argue that vowel alternations can be conditioned by coda/sonority rather than tone alone. Duan & Zhu (2025) propose eight tones across modal and lower/breathy registers. These analyses remain separate until the full empirical evidence and target profile are adjudicated.
